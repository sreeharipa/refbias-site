How it works

Team goals: attack and defence ratings learned from 75% xG + 25% goals, with recent matches weighted more (half-life 240 days). Promoted teams start below average, and empty-stadium games get their own home term.

Scorelines: two Poisson goal distributions → a probability for every score, so W/D/L, over 2.5, both-teams-score and clean sheets always agree.

Scorers: the team's expected goals are split by each player's share of team xG when on the pitch (pulled towards the position average) × expected minutes (last 20 matches, weighted towards recent ones, × FPL injury status).
